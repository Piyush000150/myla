A letter for you

Myla, if you ever forget…

Myla, my Smiley, I want you to remember something simple: you are deeply important to me. Your smile is one of my favorite things in this world, and your caring heart is one of the biggest reasons I love having you in my life.

We met through chess, and on 31 July we started talking. We did not need some perfect movie moment to begin our story. We just started talking — and that was enough to create a place for you in my heart.

I know life can get busy. We have already had to deal with busy schedules, but even when things get difficult, I do not want to disappear when things are hard. I want to communicate, understand, be patient and keep trying.

So if you ever wonder what I want you to know, it is this: I will be your forever. I want to keep making memories with you and keep learning how to love you better.

There may not be a favorite song or one huge first memory yet. That is okay. We have so many pages left to write together.

I don't want you to feel pressured to be perfect for me. I just want you to be yourself, to feel safe being honest with me, and to know that you can always talk to me about what's on your heart. Whatever life brings us, I hope we always remember why we chose to be part of each other's lives.

Until then, let's keep writing our story—one memory, one conversation, and one smile at a time.

Always yours,
Piyush ♡
